# Scoring rules (fixed in advance)

Each question scored 0–100 on nine axes. Axes that do not apply to a question
(e.g. historical understanding on a pure orientation question) are marked `n/a`
and excluded from that question's mean.

A claim is scored under C4 as:
- **SUPPORTED** — traceable to specific retrieved evidence
- **PLAUSIBLE** — consistent with evidence but not established by it
- **UNRESOLVED** — evidence insufficient either way
- **CONTRADICTED** — evidence points the other way

Presence in a retrieved neighborhood is **not** evidence of causation. A commit
touching a subsystem is **not** proof that subsystem caused a behavior. Any
causal claim resting only on co-occurrence is a **C4 violation** and caps that
question's causal-discipline score at 25 regardless of whether the named file
happens to be correct.
